About

I’m a front-end developer who likes the unglamorous parts of the web: semantic markup, sensible CSS, and pages that load before you notice them. I’ve spent the last few years building product interfaces for small teams, where one person often owns design, code and the bug report.

Away from the keyboard I cook too much, cycle the canals of Thonburi, and keep a notebook of interface details I’d like to steal.
